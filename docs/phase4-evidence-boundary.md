# Phase 4 evidence boundary

A checksum verifies captured bytes. An Ed25519 attestation verifies that one provenance key signed a bundle identity. A registry allowlist expresses local trust in that key. None of these mechanisms authenticate the original market source, prove legal identity, establish realized PnL, or predict future performance.

Out-of-sample aggregation reports registration coverage and execution-simulation evidence. It leaves candidate selection, realized PnL, Sharpe, and alpha decay unset.
